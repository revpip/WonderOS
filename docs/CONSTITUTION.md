# WonderOS Constitution

> Technology will change. Curiosity will not.

## Knowledge before content

WonderOS stores knowledge and reusable editorial assets. Pages and posts are projections.

## Provenance is sacred

Important statements must retain their sources, evidence, review state and history.

## AI proposes; editors decide

AI is an instrument. It cannot independently verify canonical truth, approve evidence or publish publicly.

## Relationships create understanding

Entities gain value through contextual, attributable and reviewable relationships.

## Every contribution should compound

Research, interviews, photographs, claims and editorial work should strengthen more than one output wherever appropriate.

## Stewardship never ends

Knowledge has a lifecycle. It is reviewed, corrected, enriched and preserved rather than discarded.

## Simplicity wins

Complexity belongs behind clear interfaces and explicit contracts. Cleverness is never a substitute for maintainability.

## Build for decades

Frameworks, models and publishing systems are replaceable. Identity, provenance and institutional memory must endure.

## Wonder exists to increase understanding

Volume, automation and traffic are not ends in themselves. The platform succeeds when editors work better and readers understand more.
